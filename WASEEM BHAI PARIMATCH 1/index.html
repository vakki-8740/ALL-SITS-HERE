Quick Answers

Why is my deposit not showing in game balance?

Deposits usually reflect within 1-3 minutes. If delayed, check UTR status with your bank & file complaint via "COMPLAIN NOW" button.

How long does withdrawal take?

UPI withdrawals: 5-15 mins | Bank Transfer: 1-24 hrs. Delays? Share your request ID via support form.

Is my password safe here?

Yes! We use end-to-end encryption. Passwords are only used to verify your account & never stored in plain text.
