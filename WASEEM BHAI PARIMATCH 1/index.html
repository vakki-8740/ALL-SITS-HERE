Quick answers

Why is my deposit not showing in game balance?
Deposits usually reflect within 1-3 minutes. If delayed, check the UTR status with your bank and file a complaint using the Deposit problem card above.
How long does a withdrawal take?
UPI withdrawals take 5-15 minutes. Bank transfers can take 1-24 hours. If the delay is longer, share your request ID through the support form.
Is my password safe here?
Your details are transferred over an encrypted connection and are only used to verify your account. They are never stored in plain text.
