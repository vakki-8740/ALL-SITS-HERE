Frequently Asked Questions

How to deposit money?

Go to Deposit section, select your payment method, enter the amount and confirm. Your money will be added instantly.

How to withdraw money?

Go to Withdrawal section, select your payment method, enter the amount and confirm. Withdrawal takes 24-48 hours.

How to verify email?

Check your email inbox for verification link. Click the link to verify. If not received, click Resend Email.

Is my account secure?

Yes, we use advanced encryption to keep your account and data 100% secure.

How to contact support?

You can contact us via Online Chat, Email or Phone. Our support team is available 24/7.
